# Decision Register — SolarSelect

This register documents all major Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) and scientific choices established throughout the SolarSelect project lifecycle.

---

## Chronological Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

| Decision ID | Session / Date | Title / Decision | Core Rationale & Supporting Evidence | Alternatives Considered | Status | Affected Files & Phases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ADR-001** | Session 1 (Phase 1) | **Raw Data Immutability** | Prevent irreproducible ad-hoc edits to sensor logs; establish auditable data lineage. | In-place cleaning of raw CSVs | **ADOPTED** | `data/raw/`, `docs/data/data_governance_and_provenance.md` |
| **ADR-002** | Session 5 (Phase 1) | **UNISOLAR Dataset Supplementation** | Original Kaggle data lacked multi-year coverage and nameplate capacity ($\text{kW}_p$), preventing specific yield ($Y_f$) modeling. UNISOLAR provided 42 sites over multi-year operations. | Cyprus PV dataset (rejected due to missing irradiance/metadata); Kaggle-only | **ADOPTED** | `data/raw/unisolar/`, Phase 1 & 2 |
| **ADR-003** | Session 15 (Phase 2) | **Prohibition Against Cross-Dataset Tabular Pooling** | Mathematical audit proved Kaggle (kW instantaneous, on-site irradiance, tropical) and UNISOLAR (15-min kWh, off-site proxy weather, temperate) suffer semantic target mismatch and covariate shift. | Joint tabular concatenation with indicator flag (rejected as unphysical) | **ADOPTED** | `notebooks/eda2.0/consolidated_eda_findings_and_dataset_selection.ipynb` |
| **ADR-004** | Session 18 (Phase 3) | **Daily Specific Yield ($Y_f$) as Primary ML Target** | Normalizing daily energy by installed capacity ($\text{kWh}/\text{kW}_p/\text{day}$) allows cross-site learning and directly powers Layer B panel sizing. | Instantaneous kW (high noise); Raw daily kWh (site-size biased); Capacity factor (equivalent) | **ADOPTED** | `src/prepare_phase3_data.py`, Phase 3–5 |
| **ADR-005** | Session 19 (Phase 3) | **A-Priori Valid Site-Day Filter ($\ge 26/28$ Core Intervals)** | Nighttime and sub-threshold intervals stored as `NaN`. Valid operational days require full 96 timestamps and $\ge 26$ non-null readings during 09:00–15:45 solar window. Sub-threshold missingness is an unresolved limitation handled by this threshold. | Dropping all `NaN` rows (distorts daily sum); Naive zero-fill of dropped intervals | **ADOPTED** | `src/prepare_phase3_data.py`, Phase 3 |
| **ADR-006** | Session 20 (Phase 3) | **Retention of Site 19 and 24 Without Arbitrary Clipping** | Site 19 ($Y_f = 18.21$) and Site 24 ($Y_f = 6.08$) exhibit severe scaling anomalies. Feeder aggregation remains an unproven hypothesis. Data preserved intact without clipping to prevent subjective tampering. | Deleting Site 19/24; Winsorizing/clipping yields to 7.0 (rejected as unprincipled) | **ADOPTED** | `data/processed/unisolar/`, Phase 3 |
| **ADR-007** | Session 20 (Phase 3) | **Dual-Cohort Architecture (Full 24-Site vs. Peer 22-Site)** | Isolates anomalous Sites 19 & 24 into Dataset A (Full Cohort) for macro tracking, while providing a clean, physically bounded Dataset B (Peer Cohort) for Layer B panel recommendations. | Single 24-site dataset (pollutes peer models); Permanent deletion of sites (loses telemetry) | **ADOPTED** | `src/prepare_phase3_data.py`, Phase 3–5 |
| **ADR-008** | Session 21 (Phase 3) | **Strict Calendar-Aware Day-Ahead Lags ($\tau \ge 24\text{h}$)** | Eliminates contemporaneous weather leakage. Shifts executed on continuous calendar grid to prevent forward-fill distortion across missing dates. | Contemporaneous weather (causes test leakage); Simple row-index shift (distorts gaps) | **ADOPTED** | `src/prepare_phase3_data.py`, Phase 3 |
| **ADR-009** | Session 22 (Phase 3) | **Separation of Diagnostic Metadata from Feature Matrix** | Diagnostic indicators (e.g. interval counts, capacity factors) contain target leakage and must never be accessed by predictive models. | Keeping all variables in single table (high leakage risk) | **ADOPTED** | `data/processed/unisolar/diagnostic_metadata.csv` |
| **ADR-010** | Session 24 (Phase 4) | **Expanding-Window Temporal CV Grouped by Date** | Time series data violates i.i.d. assumptions. Expanding window respects chronological directionality; date grouping prevents same-day cross-site snooping. | K-fold randomized CV (severe leakage); Block time series CV without date grouping | **ADOPTED** | `notebooks/phase4/baseline_and_model_development.ipynb` |
| **ADR-011** | Session 27 (Phase 5) | **Strict Test-Set Quarantine Until Prompt 3** | Preserved held-out partition (`2022-03-01` to `2022-04-23`) completely untouched through Phases 1–4 and Phase 5 Prompts 1–2. Evaluated exactly once on frozen models. | Repeated validation on test set (induces subtle test-set overfitting) | **ADOPTED** | `notebooks/phase5/README.md`, Phase 5 |
| **ADR-012** | Session 28 (Phase 5) | **Rejection of Astronomical Post-Hoc Calibration & Site Bias** | Evaluated in 4-fold CV: Delta RMSE was $<0.0012$. Astronomical inputs already in 29 features; site-bias added stateful complexity with zero practical gain. | Adopting post-hoc residual scaling without CV justification | **ADOPTED** | `notebooks/phase5/model_improvement_experiments.ipynb` |
| **ADR-013** | Session 29 (Phase 5) | **Final Offline Evaluated Model Acceptance Decisions** | Tuned ExtraTrees **ACCEPTED** for Peer Cohort (beats persistence by 17% MAE, positive $R^2$); Tuned ElasticNet **CONDITIONALLY ACCEPTED** for Full Cohort (linear handling of Site 19). | Rejecting ExtraTrees due to summer validation bias; Accepting ElasticNet unconditionally | **ADOPTED** | `models/phase5/final/`, Phase 5 Prompt 3 |
| **ADR-014** | Session 30 (Phase 6) | **Catalogue Canonical Schema & Data-Source Governance** | Established 43-column schema and automated validator. Banned web scraping, unapproved downloads, or parameter guessing. Formal datasheet request submitted. | Web scraping manufacturer sites; Synthetic parameter generation; Ad-hoc schema | **ADOPTED** | `configs/panels_catalogue_schema.json`, Phase 6 |
| **ADR-015** | Session 32 (Phase 6 Prompt 2) | **Official Datasheet Ingestion & 12-Module Catalogue Expansion** | Ingested official manufacturer PDF datasheets into `data/reference/panel_datasheets/`. Established 12-row `source_manifest.csv` with SHA-256 integrity hashes and 516-row `field_provenance.csv`. Verified all 3 campus baselines (Trina 330W and 310W classified as candidate installation mappings; SunPower E20 confirmed) and 9 commercial modules across 6 distinct semiconductor technologies. Pricing fields maintained explicitly null (`NaN`) per strict provenance policy. | Web scraping; synthetic pricing; unverified third-party aggregator data | **ADOPTED** | `configs/panels_catalogue.csv`, `data/reference/panel_datasheets/`, Phase 6 Prompt 2 |
| **ADR-016** | Session 37 (Phase 7 Prompt 1) | **Geometric Coordinate Standards & Physical Separation of Concerns** | Established SI metres ($m, m^2$) and 2D Cartesian frame $(X, Y)$ with bottom-left origin for Phase 7. Strictly decoupled physical geometric fit ($N_{\text{max}}, P_{\text{DC}}$) from electrical inverter/MPPT matching (Phase 8), specific yield prediction (Phase 5), and financial LCOE modeling (Phase 8). Mandated effective installation footprint distinction ($L_e = L_m + G_x, W_e = W_m + G_y$) vs bare module glass dimensions. | Millimetres/feet units; Coupling string electrical sizing directly into physical 2D fit engine; Treating bare module area as installed footprint | **ADOPTED** | `docs/engineering/phase7_requirements.md`, `docs/engineering/phase7_architecture.md`, Phase 7 |
| **ADR-017** | Session 37 (Phase 7 Prompt 1) | **Selection of Row-Wise Strip Packing with Obstacle Interruption** | Evaluated 5 packing paradigms. Selected Row-Wise Strip Packing because it directly models linear racking rail collinearity. Rejects isolated/floating placements generated by pure bin-packers (MaxRects). Generates geometrically feasible placements under supplied parameters without claiming structural safety, regulatory compliance, installation approval, or unproven global optimality (designated 'Best Feasible Configuration Found'). | Uniform Grid (too rigid around obstacles); Maximal Rectangles (generates non-linear un-rackable layouts); Skyline / Guillotine (asymmetrical / destructive cuts) | **ADOPTED** | `docs/engineering/phase7_algorithm_comparison.md`, `docs/engineering/phase7_input_output_contract.md`, Phase 7 |
