# Assumptions Register — SolarSelect

This register records all scientific, modeling, operational, and data assumptions made in SolarSelect, distinguishing assumptions from verified empirical facts.

---

## Authoritative Assumptions Register

| Assumption ID | Statement | Evidence | Current Classification | Confidence | Impact if Incorrect | Required Follow-Up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ASM-001** | **Sub-threshold generation intervals recorded as `NaN` can be treated as zero in valid site-day energy summation.** | Night intervals and values $<0.100\text{ kWh}$ are stored as `NaN`. Missingness correlates strongly with darkness hours. However, telemetry dropouts during daylight are also stored as `NaN`. | **Partially Supported / Unresolved Limitation** | Medium | If `NaN` represents unrecorded daytime generation, daily sums are underestimated. If missingness represents zero, summation is valid. | Maintain $\ge 26/28$ core daylight interval filter; conduct sensor-level dropout audits if inverter logs become available. |
| **ASM-002** | **Documented DC nameplate capacity ($\text{kW}_p$) in `Solar_Site_Details.csv` accurately reflects installed array capacity under STC.** | Sourced from university facilities management documentation. Verified consistent across 22 peer sites. | **Falsified for Sites 19 & 24; Partially Supported for Peer Cohort** | High (Peer) / Zero (Sites 19 & 24) | Distorts specific yield calculation ($Y_f = E / \text{kW}_p$) if capacity is wrong, causing artificial scaling anomalies. | Physical on-site array audit for Sites 19 & 24; maintain dual-cohort isolation (Datasets A & B). |
| **ASM-003** | **Site 19 excessive output is caused by unmetered building feeder aggregation.** | Equivalent average interval power reaches $168.88\text{ kW}$ ($492.06\%$ of $34.32\text{ kW}_p$ STC rating and $611.88\%$ of $27.6\text{ kW}$ inverter rating). Panels are standard Trina 330W. | **Unproven Hypothesis** | Low | If Site 19 is an undocumented high-capacity ground array or concentrator, the feeder hypothesis is false. | Single-line diagram electrical audit of campus distribution board. Retained without clipping. |
| **ASM-004** | **Regional BOM automated weather stations provide suitable ambient weather proxies for campus rooftop generation.** | Bureau of Meteorology automated stations are within 15 km of campus. Ambient temperature and humidity correlate with seasonal solar output. | **Partially Supported** | Medium | Localized microclimates (cloud cover, urban heat island) weaken coupling ($r \approx 0.19$ vs on-site irradiance $r \approx 0.98$). | Integrate satellite or on-site pyranometer irradiance if available in future data collection. |
| **ASM-005** | **Operational day-ahead numerical weather predictions (NWP) will exhibit similar error bounds to historical BOM observations.** | Regional day-ahead forecast models (BOM ACCESS / GFS) predict temperature and humidity with known standard errors. | **Pending Future Testing** | Medium | Higher NWP forecast errors will directly propagate into degraded real-time yield forecast accuracy. | Evaluate forecasting models with archived historical day-ahead NWP forecasts in Phase 9 MLOps serving. |
| **ASM-006** | **Substituting training medians for single-day lag dropouts provides an operational heuristic for missing lag inputs.** | Autoregressive features require continuous history. When day $t-1$ telemetry is missing, training median provides a neutral central estimate. Flagged via `has_valid_lag1 = 0`. | **Heuristic Operational Assumption** | Medium | If missing lag periods carry structural bias (e.g. multi-day storms causing outages), median imputation could bias predictions. | Avoid using held-out test data to justify preprocessing; evaluate imputation policies in cross-validation experiments. |
| **ASM-007** | **Commercial PV modules conform to manufacturer datasheet power and degradation tolerances under IEC standards.** | IEC 61215 / IEC 61730 international certification standards require factory flash tests to adhere to documented tolerances (typically $0$ to $+5\text{ W}$). | **Supported / Industry Standard (Audited in Phase 6 Prompt 2)** | High | Substandard or degraded modules will deliver less energy than simulated in Layer B. | Ingest only certified official datasheets; require tolerance parameters in catalogue schema. Verified across 12 manufacturer datasheets. |
| **ASM-008** | **Exclusion of speculative commercial pricing from initial catalogue records maintains scientific integrity without impairing technical sizing.** | Solar panel market prices fluctuate rapidly by region, procurement volume, and distributor margins; manufacturer technical datasheets omit pricing. | **Supported Operational Policy** | High | Fabricated or guessed prices would bias financial ranking in Layer B. | Maintain explicit nulls (`NaN`) in catalogue until audited invoices or formal distributor price lists are ingested. |
