# Limitations Register — SolarSelect

This register records all documented empirical, physical, algorithmic, and operational limitations identified across the SolarSelect platform.

---

## Authoritative Limitations Register

| Limitation ID | Category | Specific Limitation | Empirical Evidence / Manifestation | Affected Functionality | Severity | Mitigation / Current Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LIM-001** | Data Quality | **Site 19 Extreme Scaling Anomaly** | 15-minute recorded energy reaches $42.22\text{ kWh}$, representing an equivalent average interval power of $168.88\text{ kW}$ ($492.06\%$ of $34.32\text{ kW}_p$ STC rating and $611.88\%$ of $27.6\text{ kW}$ inverter rating). Feeder aggregation is an unproven hypothesis. | Full Cohort model training and evaluation | **HIGH** | Excluded completely from Peer Cohort (Dataset B). Retained in Full Cohort with documented conditional status; prohibited for panel recommendations. |
| **LIM-002** | Data Quality | **Site 24 Capacity Mismatch Anomaly** | Observed yield averages $6.08\text{ kWh}/\text{kW}_p/\text{day}$. Peak 15-minute interval energy ($16.59\text{ kWh}$) represents an equivalent average interval power of $66.36\text{ kW}$ ($16.59 / 0.25$), exceeding panel nameplate ($40.26\text{ kW}_p$, $164.8\%$) but within inverter capacity ($82.8\text{ kW}$, $80.1\%$). (Supersedes preliminary $54.52\text{ kW}$ figure). Root cause unconfirmed. | Full Cohort model training | **MEDIUM** | Retained at corrected Phase 3 capacity ($40.26\text{ kW}_p$). Excluded from Peer Cohort; retained in Full Cohort. |
| **LIM-003** | Meteorology | **Absence of On-Site Pyranometer Irradiance** | Primary UNISOLAR dataset lacks measured on-site solar irradiance ($G_{\text{poa}}$). Regional BOM weather proxies correlate moderately with generation ($r \approx 0.19$ to $0.35$). | Feature engineering & physical modeling | **MEDIUM** | Engineered analytical extraterrestrial radiation ($H_0$), daylight hours, and temperature ranges as proxies. |
| **LIM-004** | Meteorology | **Absence of Archived Day-Ahead NWP Forecasts** | Realized historical weather observations were used during model development rather than historical NWP forecasts. | Real-world operational transition | **MEDIUM** | Operational deployment must integrate an external weather forecast API (e.g. Open-Meteo, GFS, BOM). |
| **LIM-005** | Modeling | **Seasonal Underprediction Bias on Tree Ensembles** | When trained on winter/spring only, ExtraTrees underpredicted summer validation by $0.8338\text{ kWh}/\text{kW}_p/\text{day}$ due to leaf-averaging bounds. | Validation performance & seasonal transfer | **RESOLVED IN FINAL FIT** | Resolved in offline evaluated model by retraining on combined pre-test data (Train + Val); test bias fell to $-0.1036$. |
| **LIM-006** | Modeling | **Spatial Non-Transferability (Geographic Bound)** | Telemetry collected strictly on Campus 1 in Melbourne, Australia ($-37.7^\circ\text{ S}$). Evaluated across time on known sites, not unseen geographic locations. | Cross-geographic deployment | **HIGH** | Models cannot be deployed in equatorial or alternate climate zones without local telemetry retraining. |
| **LIM-007** | Modeling | **Temporal Variance Compression in Late Autumn** | April test set cross-sectional target variance compressed to $1.18$, causing nominal $R^2$ to dip to $-0.0401$, despite low absolute error ($\text{MAE} = 0.8967$). | Late autumn metric reporting | **LOW** | Absolute tracking error remained low; acknowledged as a target variance sensitivity artifact. |
| **LIM-008** | Catalogue | **Absence of Verified Commercial Datasheets in Codebase** | Active repository previously contained 0 verified manufacturer datasheets; Appendix B's 14 models were documentation claims only. | Layer B roof-fit and recommendation engine | **RESOLVED IN PHASE 6 PROMPT 2 (WITH DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS)** | Ingested technical specifications for 12 commercial modules across 6 distinct technologies into canonical catalogue and local compiled reference summaries. Primary-source re-verification governed under LIM-012 and LIM-013. |
| **LIM-009** | Catalogue | **Zero Commercial Pricing Data in Repository** | Active catalogue contains 0 pricing records. Official datasheets do not publish retail/wholesale prices. | Financial LCOE and payback optimization | **GOVERNED** | Commercial pricing maintained explicitly null (`NaN`) to prevent speculative inputs. Technical sizing proceeds unhindered; commercial pricing pending authorized quote ingestion. |
| **LIM-010** | Statistics | **Prohibition on Using Test-Set Residuals for Uncertainty Calibration** | Test-set residuals cannot be used to fit prediction intervals without violating statistical independence. | Layer B uncertainty estimation | **GOVERNED** | Mandated that Phase 8 uncertainty intervals must be calibrated strictly via cross-validation out-of-fold residuals. |
| **LIM-011** | Data Quality | **Unresolved UNISOLAR Missing-Value Representation** | Telemetry contains substantial `NaN` values concentrated at night and below $0.100–0.125\text{ kWh}$. Available evidence cannot distinguish all sub-threshold output from telemetry dropout. | Daily energy summation | **MEDIUM** | Filter site-days by requiring $\ge 26/28$ core daylight intervals; do not naively replace all `NaN` values with zero. |
| **LIM-012** | Catalogue | **Compiled Technical Specification Summary Format** | The 12 local PDF files in `data/reference/panel_datasheets/` are single-page standards-compliant PDF 1.4 technical specification summaries compiled by `src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py` (~3.4 KB each) rather than original multi-megabyte marketing or engineering datasheet PDFs downloaded directly from manufacturer CDNs. | Audit trails & documentary inspection | **DOCUMENTED LIMITATION** | Catalogue values and compiled reference summaries are retained as reference evidence. SHA-256 digests establish local file integrity against `source_manifest.csv`, not primary manufacturer-source authenticity. Classified transparently as `REDUCED_COMPILED_SPECIFICATION_DOCUMENT`. |
| **LIM-013** | Catalogue | **Manufacturer Datasheet Source Inaccessibility & Pending Primary Re-Verification** | 100% of recorded source URLs point to official manufacturer corporate domains, but live network testing revealed the documents to be inaccessible (9 HTTP 404s, 1 HTML redirect, 2 network timeouts) due to manufacturer portal restructuring and link rot. | Independent primary-source re-verification | **DOCUMENTED LIMITATION** | The catalogue retains 370 technical specification provenance entries represented in locally generated, compiled reference summaries and attributed to manufacturer documentation. The original manufacturer source documents were not independently accessible during the final audit. Consequently, primary-source re-verification of these entries, including exact variant-level and page-level evidence, remains a documented limitation. |
| **LIM-014** | Geometry | **2D Planar Geometric Scope & Structural/Wind Safety Disclaimer** | Phase 7 models 2D planar rectangular geometry with setbacks and obstacles. It does not model 3D roof structural dead load capacity ($kg/m^2$), rafter deflection, wind uplifting pressures (AS/NZS 1170.2 / ASCE 7), or electrical service capacity. Initial engine does not certify fire-code, wind-load, or statutory compliance. | Real-world physical installation approval | **GOVERNED** | The engine generates geometrically feasible panel placements under the supplied roof geometry, module dimensions, clearances and obstacle assumptions. These results do not establish structural safety, regulatory compliance or installation approval. Physical installation requires independent certified civil structural engineering and licensed electrical contractor approval. |
