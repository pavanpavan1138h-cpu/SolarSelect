## 1. Objective

The objective of this task is to execute the final targeted documentation corrections for Phase 6 (Real Solar Panel Catalogue) following the forensic findings established in [`reports/phase6/phase6_final_datasheet_authenticity_verification.md`](phase6_final_datasheet_authenticity_verification.md).

The forensic audit confirmed that the 12 local PDF files stored in [`data/reference/panel_datasheets/`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/) are **single-page, compiled technical specification summaries** generated by [`src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py`](../../src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py) (~3.4 KB each), rather than original multi-megabyte marketing or engineering datasheet PDFs downloaded from manufacturer CDNs. Furthermore, live HTTP/HTTPS testing confirmed that the original manufacturer source URLs are currently inaccessible or redirecting due to manufacturer portal restructuring and link rot.

This task resolves all conflicting verification claims across Phase 6 documentation, establishes strict epistemic distinctions between retained catalogue values, compiled reference summaries, and pending primary-source re-verification, updates the living registers, and formally closes Phase 6 under the explicit status **`COMPLETE_WITH_DOCUMENTED_LIMITATIONS`**.

## 3. Evidence-Status Corrections Made

To prevent misleading claims of unconditional external verification while preserving the validity of the technical database, the documentation now strictly maintains a tripartite epistemic distinction:

1. **Catalogue Values:**
   - The 12 records in [`configs/panels_catalogue.csv`](../../configs/panels_catalogue.csv) and their 43 attributes are fully retained. All numerical values satisfy physical and electrical formulas ($P_{\text{mp}} = V_{\text{mp}} \times I_{\text{mp}}$, $\eta = P_{\text{mp}} / (A \times 1000)$, $V_{\text{oc}} > V_{\text{mp}}$, $I_{\text{sc}} > I_{\text{mp}}$) and engineering consistency checks.
2. **Compiled Reference Evidence:**
   - The 12 local single-page PDF files in [`data/reference/panel_datasheets/`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/) are structured reference summaries compiled by [`src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py`](../../src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py). They are retained as compiled reference evidence. SHA-256 digests confirm bit-for-bit local file integrity against [`source_manifest.csv`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/source_manifest.csv), verifying that local artifacts have not been modified or corrupted.
3. **Primary-Source Manufacturer Verification:**
   - Live HTTP/HTTPS testing established that original manufacturer source documents are currently inaccessible, unavailable, or redirecting (9 HTTP 404s, 1 HTML corporate redirect, 2 network timeouts). Consequently, independent primary-source re-verification against original manufacturer documents, including exact variant-level and page-level evidence, remains a documented limitation.

### Specific Evidence Statement Applied Across Documentation
> *The catalogue retains 370 technical specification provenance entries represented in locally generated, compiled reference summaries and attributed to manufacturer documentation. The original manufacturer source documents were not independently accessible during the final verification audit. Consequently, primary-source re-verification of these entries, including exact variant-level and page-level evidence, remains a documented limitation.*

## 4. Terminology Corrections Made

The following terminology substitutions were applied across the documentation base:

| Obsolete / Imprecise Terminology | Standardized, Accurate Terminology | Context & Justification |
|---|---|---|
| "Original manufacturer datasheets" (for local files) | **"Compiled technical specification summaries"** or **"Compiled reference PDFs"** | Acknowledges that local PDF files were compiled by `src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py` (~3.4 KB each) rather than downloaded directly from manufacturer CDNs. |
| "Unconditionally verified against official datasheets" | **"Manufacturer-attributed technical specifications retained in compiled reference summaries"** | Reflects that specifications are drawn from manufacturer product lines but primary URLs were inaccessible during the final audit. |
| "Verified source URLs" (implying active downloads) | **"Original manufacturer source document — currently inaccessible"** | Preserves official manufacturer URLs in `source_manifest.csv` while transparently recording live link rot. |
| "Cryptographically verified manufacturer origin" | **"SHA-256 local file integrity verified against source manifest"** | Clarifies that SHA-256 hashing guarantees local file immutability, not external manufacturer CDN provenance. |
