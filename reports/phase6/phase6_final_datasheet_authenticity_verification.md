## 1. Audit Objective & Forensic Context

This forensic audit was initiated following the observation that all 12 PDF files stored in [`data/reference/panel_datasheets/`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/) exhibit compact file sizes (between 3,343 and 3,476 bytes each). In commercial engineering practice, original multi-page marketing or engineering datasheets published by solar manufacturers typically range from several hundred kilobytes to several megabytes, containing vector schematics, high-resolution branding, and multi-language disclosures.

To ensure uncompromising scientific integrity and clear documentation boundaries, this audit systematically distinguishes three epistemic layers:
1. **Catalogue Values:** The technical parameters currently populated and retained in [`configs/panels_catalogue.csv`](../../configs/panels_catalogue.csv).
2. **Compiled Reference Evidence:** The values represented in the locally stored, single-page compiled technical specification summaries generated by the project compiler ([`src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py`](../../src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py)).
3. **Primary-Source Manufacturer Verification:** Independent, reproducible verification against original manufacturer source documents, including exact variant-level and page-level evidence.

### Fundamental Audit Questions & Empirical Findings
1. **What is the true physical and structural nature of the 12 local PDF files?**
   - The files are single-page, standards-compliant PDF 1.4 compiled technical specification summaries generated by [`src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py`](../../src/acquire_phase6_datasheets.py). They are not original manufacturer marketing or engineering brochure PDFs downloaded directly from manufacturer CDNs.
2. **Are the recorded manufacturer URLs authentic and accessible?**
   - All 12 source URLs point to legitimate, manufacturer-owned corporate domains (`trinasolar.com`, `us.sunpower.com`, `csisolar.com`, `jinkosolar.com`, `recgroup.com`, `firstsolar.com`, `waaree.com`, `maxeon.com`, `longi-solar.com`). However, live network requests established that none of the direct document URLs are currently accessible (9 return HTTP 404, 1 returns an HTML corporate redirect, and 2 time out).
3. **What is the status of the 370 technical specification provenance rows?**
   - The catalogue retains 370 technical specification provenance entries represented in locally generated, compiled reference summaries and attributed to manufacturer documentation. The original manufacturer source documents were not independently accessible during the final verification audit. Consequently, primary-source re-verification of these entries, including exact variant-level and page-level evidence, remains a documented limitation.
4. **Does SHA-256 hashing verify manufacturer origin?**
   - Cryptographic SHA-256 verification confirms 100% bit-for-bit local file integrity against [`source_manifest.csv`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/source_manifest.csv). This confirms that local files have not been modified or corrupted since generation; it does not establish independent primary manufacturer-source authenticity.

### Inspection Methodology & Tooling
The forensic investigation utilized deterministic local tools and live network probes:
- **Structural Analysis:** `/opt/homebrew/bin/pdfinfo` (Poppler) for metadata, page dimensions, PDF specifications, encryption status, and stream structures.
- **Textual Extraction:** `/opt/homebrew/bin/pdftotext` to extract raw stream contents and verify character fidelity against IEC standards.
- **Cryptographic Hashing:** Python `hashlib.sha256()` byte-stream evaluation against [`data/reference/panel_datasheets/source_manifest.csv`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/source_manifest.csv).
- **Network Probe:** Python `urllib.request` / `ssl` client testing HTTP/HTTPS connection states, headers, redirection targets, and MIME types.
- **Provenance Matrix Audit:** Vectorized Pandas evaluation of all 516 records in [`data/reference/panel_datasheets/field_provenance.csv`](../../data/reference/panel_datasheets/field_provenance.csv) against canonical schema boundaries.

### 1. Site 24 Peak Power Reconciliation
- **Authoritative Phase 3 Record:** In [`data/raw/unisolar/Solar_Energy_Generation.csv`](../../data/raw/unisolar/Solar_Energy_Generation.csv) and [`data/processed/unisolar/preprocessing_metadata.json`](../../data/processed/unisolar/preprocessing_metadata.json), the maximum recorded 15-minute interval energy for Site 24 is **$16.59\text{ kWh}$**.
- **Physical Interval Average Power:**
  $$\bar{P}_{\text{interval}} = \frac{E_{\text{interval}}}{\Delta t} = \frac{16.59\text{ kWh}}{0.25\text{ h}} = 66.36\text{ kW}$$
- **Origin of Preliminary Reference:** A preliminary calculation referenced $13.63\text{ kWh} / 0.25\text{ h} = 54.52\text{ kW}$. The empirical telemetry confirms $16.59\text{ kWh}$ ($66.36\text{ kW}$) as the true historical maximum.
- **Physical Inverter & Panel Capacity Analysis:**
  - Site 24 STC panel rating: $40.26\text{ kW}_p$ (122 modules $\times 330\text{ W}$; $66.36\text{ kW} = 164.83\%$ of DC nameplate).
  - Site 24 Inverter rating: SolarEdge SE82.8K ($82.8\text{ kW}_{\text{AC}}$).
  - Inverter utilization: $66.36 / 82.8 = 80.14\%$.
  - **Conclusion:** While $66.36\text{ kW}$ significantly exceeds the DC panel nameplate rating (consistent with documented anomaly LIM-002), it does not exceed the AC inverter capacity ($82.8\text{ kW}$). The obsolete $33.15\text{ kW}_p$ capacity is fully superseded.
