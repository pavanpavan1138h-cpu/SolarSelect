## 1. The Three Issues Addressed

During the final review of the Phase 6 closure deliverables, three specific documentation issues were identified and addressed:

1. **Qualification of the "Fabricated-Values" Claim:**
   - Previous audit statements stating "0 fabricated values" or implying that the validation engine independently proves external manufacturer authenticity were overly broad. The claim required qualification to reflect that the checks performed were structural and engineering consistency checks.
2. **Clarification of the Validator's Documentary Status:**
   - The status label `TECHNICAL_SPECIFICATIONS_VERIFIED_WITH_CANDIDATE_CAMPUS_MAPPINGS` generated by [`src/validate_panel_catalogue.py`](../../src/validate_panel_catalogue.py) risked being interpreted as asserting external primary-source document authentication. The associated documentation required explicit clarification that the validator evaluates catalogue schema conformance and physical engineering consistency against retained compiled specification records, rather than independently authenticating inaccessible original manufacturer documents.
3. **Replacement of Machine-Specific File Links:**
   - Markdown documents updated during Phase 6 closure contained machine-specific URIs using the `file://` scheme. These links required replacement with portable relative Markdown links calculated from each document's actual path, ensuring full cross-platform portability.
